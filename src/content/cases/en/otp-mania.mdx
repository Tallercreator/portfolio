## In short

A storefront where employees spend the points they've earned on goods and merch: catalogue, product page, cart, orders, virtual goods, mascot.

The catalogue and cart logic came out of interviews with employees. The mechanics look like an ordinary online shop, but the currency is internal and you can't get it back. That made order confirmation and a legible points balance matter more than a fast checkout.

The least obvious problem showed up with managers. A manager has two balances: their own, and the one they use to reward their team. There was a switcher between the two and it was labelled — “manager balance”, “employee balance” — and people still lost track of whose points they were spending. We kept the switcher and put both balances on the home screen, separately. A label on a switcher tells you where you are only if you looked at it.

The module is in pilot.
