## Context and problem

Salary arrives as a single figure but is assembled from many parts: base pay, bonuses, supplements, deductions. People see the total and not how it was reached. Benefits sit separately, and often nobody knows they exist — they are there, but you only find out by asking.

## Solution

Three parts tied together by one logic: pay structure, a visual breakdown of the salary calculation, and benefits. I designed the mobile version from the start rather than adapting it afterwards — people check their pay on a phone more often than at a work computer.
