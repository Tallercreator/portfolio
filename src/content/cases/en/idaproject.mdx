## In short

A website and personal dashboards for a proptech platform. Three kinds of user, each wanting something different: the client is picking a flat, the broker is running deals and watching their commission, the developer is looking at sales as a whole. The underlying data is shared — the same property looks different depending on who is looking at it.

<Note kind="fork">
We could have built three separate dashboards, which makes it easier to negotiate with each audience on its own terms. I went with one product on a shared component system: three interfaces over the same data drift apart quickly, and one small team would have had to maintain all of them.

What stayed different was the navigation and what you see first when you log in. Cards, tables, filters and forms are shared.
</Note>

Alongside it I built a design system from scratch: tokens, components, guidelines, layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile. I defended the decisions to b2b clients, gathered requirements with analysts, reviewed other people's files and onboarded junior designers.
